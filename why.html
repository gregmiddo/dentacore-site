02 / 08 · Ways to buy & more

Everyone opens to the screens they use most

Every role

Each person signs in with their own role and lands on the screens their day is built around.

  • Reception: the Dashboard, the Appointment Book, patients, billing and recalls. A shared front-desk login still asks who is at the desk.
  • Dentists: Chairside view with who's next, today's patients, their chart, images and notes, plus their own day sheet and numbers.
  • Assistants and nurses: the waiting room, visit steps at the chair, charting help, image capture and room prep for each procedure.
  • Practice managers and owners: reports, profitability, stock, the roster, users, settings, backups and the audit log. Owner-only reports stay owner-only.

03 / 08 · Ways to buy & more

Funds, Medicare, DVA and the BAS, the way they work here

Billing & Invoicing

Built for Australian practice from the first screen to the last export.

  • Health fund claims through HICAPS, with each line's item category and the gap recorded as a payment.
  • Medicare CDBS checks the patient's age on the invoice date and the two-year cap that's left.
  • DVA Gold, White and Orange cards, the prior approval reference and the no-gap rule.
  • GST on the lines that carry it, and exports to Xero or MYOB AccountRight by BAS quarter or financial year.
  • Live claiming connects through Tyro Health. Until a practice's connection is certified, claims run in a clearly labelled training mode.

04 / 08 · Ways to buy & more

Your server, your workstations, your data

How it runs

DentaCore is a Windows desktop program. Every computer in the practice, and every site over a VPN, works on one PostgreSQL database that you host, so everyone sees the same day as it changes.

  • Install in minutes: run the installer, point it at the database once, sign in. Try it first with a sample practice.
  • Several locations: book by location and room, each site on its own floor plan, one patient list.
  • Security you can check: personal logins, an idle lock, encrypted backups and a tamper-evident audit log.
  • Bring your data across: conversions for some mainstream practice systems are ready, and we can set one up for yours.
  • Help built in: F1 on any window, a guided tour, and an illustrated guide for each role.
